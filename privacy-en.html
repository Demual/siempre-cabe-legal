Privacy Policy

Siempre cabe app (com.jrsoft.puzlebloques) · Last updated: 11 November 2026

In one sentence: Siempre cabe keeps your games, your settings and your statistics on your phone. There are no accounts and no servers of its own, and none of that leaves your phone except when you share a result or the app, or save a backup. Apart from that, the only data that leaves it is the technical and advertising data collected by Google's ads library, explained below.

Who is responsible

The data controller is JR Soft, trading name of Javier Román Sáez, developer of the Siempre cabe app. You can get in touch at demual@gmail.com.

What this app does NOT do

  • There is no sign-up, account, username or password. You are never asked for an email address, a phone number, a name or your location.
  • It does not upload your games. Your games in progress, best scores, statistics, daily challenges, streak and settings are stored on your phone. This app has no servers of its own.
  • It includes no analytics or crash-reporting tools of its own (no Google Analytics, no Firebase Analytics and no Crashlytics). Google's ads library does send its technical data, explained below.
  • JR Soft does not receive or store your data (except the emails you send us) and does not hand lists or databases containing it to anyone. To show and measure ads, Google's ads SDK (AdMob) collects device identifiers and other technical data on its own and sends them to Google, as explained below. Some US privacy laws consider that letting a third party's SDK collect that data for personalised advertising is “selling” or “sharing” personal data; if you live there, you can opt out (see “US residents”).
  • It does not ask for permission to use the microphone, the camera, your files, your contacts or your location. Backup files are saved and opened through Android's screens, and only the ones you choose.
  • There are no purchases, coins, loot boxes or betting. The game is free.
  • Your data is not included in Android's backup to Google's cloud.

Permissions

  • Internet, network state and advertising identifier (AD_ID and the permissions of Android's advertising services), which the advertising needs (see below).
  • The ones Google's libraries add on their own (WAKE_LOCK and FOREGROUND_SERVICE), which give no access to any of your data.
  • Vibration and sounds need no permission. So that it does not make a sound when your phone is on silent, the app asks Android whether the ringer is on silent or vibrate; that answer does not leave the phone.

Data that stays only on your phone

This information is kept in the app's private storage. It disappears if you uninstall the app or clear its data from Android settings.

  • The game in progress in each mode (Classic, Relaxed, Daily challenge and Board of the week).
  • Your best scores and statistics: games, points, lines cleared and best streak, by mode and by week.
  • The daily challenges you have completed, with their stars, and your streak.
  • Your settings: theme, piece colors and symbols, piece distance from your finger, piece size, “Tap to place”, sound, vibration and animations.
  • The date on which the app last asked you for a rating, so that it does not ask again too soon.

Backups and switching phones. Android's cloud backup (the one in your Google account) is turned off for this app's data: none of it is uploaded. Android does keep a list of the apps you had installed in your account so it can reinstall them, but Google manages that list and it contains no data from this app.

If, while setting up a new phone, you copy your data directly from the old one, with or without a cable, the app's data may be moved to the new phone. You ask for this transfer and Android carries it out, using its own tool or your phone maker's, under their own terms. According to Android's documentation, it goes from one phone to the other without going through servers. It never goes through ours: JR Soft receives nothing.

Whether it happens depends on your Android version and phone maker (it doesn't happen on Android 8 or earlier), so we can't guarantee it. If you set up the new phone from your Google account backup without the old phone, this app's data won't come back, because it isn't in that backup. So you don't have to rely on that, you can save a backup to a file yourself (see below). Your ad choices are not transferred: the app will ask you again on the new phone.

What you choose to share

  • Sharing a daily challenge. When you finish a challenge, “Share” prepares, on your phone, an image and a text with the date, your stars and your points, the points of “the machine”, the final board in color and the Siempre cabe link on Google Play. They carry neither your name nor any data about you. They are handed to the app you choose on Android's screen (messaging, social networks, email…), and from then on that app handles them, under its own terms.
  • Sharing the app. “Share the app” (Settings → About) hands the app you choose a text with the Siempre cabe link on Google Play. The link carries a parameter that only says it comes from sharing the app, with no data about you.
  • Backup to a file. “Save a backup to a file” saves a file (siempre-cabe-YYYY-MM-DD.json) with your settings, statistics, challenges, streak and games in progress, wherever you choose, through Android's screen. If that folder syncs with a cloud (for example, Google Drive), the syncing is done by the service you chose, under its own terms. “Restore a backup” reads the file you choose, only if it is a Siempre cabe backup, and warns you beforehand of what is going to be replaced.
  • Writing to us. If you write to us at the contact address, we receive your email address and whatever you tell us. We use them only to reply to you, and we delete the message once the matter is resolved and, at the latest, one year after receiving it.

Data that does leave your phone

Advertising: Google AdMob

The app is free and is supported by one unobtrusive Google AdMob ad (a banner, silent) that only appears at the bottom of the home screen and the statistics: never during a game, when you lose, in the daily challenge, in the settings or in full screen. Ad requests carry nothing from your games.

As with any app that uses AdMob, Google's SDK collects the Android advertising identifier (you can reset or delete it in Android Settings → Google → Ads), the app set identifier (app set ID), your IP address (from which an approximate location is inferred), your interactions with the app and with the ads, and diagnostic data. Google uses this data to serve and measure advertising, for statistics and to prevent fraud, under its own policy. The app asks Google for ads suitable for teenagers at most.

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the app shows Google's consent form before serving personalized advertising. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Ad privacy options. If you decline, you will see non-personalized advertising (or limited advertising, without identifiers, if you also decline the use of device storage) and the app works exactly the same.

Google shows the ad consent message with wording that is common to all its advertising partners, which is why it mentions precise geolocation. According to Google, its ads SDK only uses the approximate location derived from your IP address, and this app does not give it any location. You can refuse that use in “Manage options”.

How Google uses information from apps that use its services: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Ratings and Google Play

Every now and then, after you finish a game with a new best score or complete the daily challenge with three stars (from the third finished game on, and at most once every four months), the app may ask you for a rating through Google Play's official window, which is managed by Google. “Rate the app” and “More apps by JR Soft” open Google Play. To show you only the apps available in your country, “More apps by JR Soft” checks, on the phone, the country of the SIM card or of the system region; that information does not leave your phone. Its links to Google Play carry a parameter that only says they come from Siempre cabe, with no data about you.

IP address

As with any app that connects to the internet, the Google services mentioned above receive your IP address when connecting. This app has no servers of its own, so it keeps no logs of any kind.

Legal basis and retention

Legal basis. What is processed on your phone does not reach JR Soft: your games and statistics are stored so that the game can do what you ask it to (Art. 6.1.b GDPR). For what does leave it:

  1. Personalized advertising and the use of device storage for advertising purposes in the EEA, the United Kingdom and Switzerland: your consent, which you give or refuse in Google's form and can withdraw in Settings → Ad privacy options (Art. 6.1.a GDPR and Art. 22.2 LSSI-CE).
  2. Non-personalized advertising, its measurement, technical logs and fraud prevention: the legitimate interest in funding a free app, keeping it working properly and protecting it from fraud (Art. 6.1.f GDPR).
  3. If you write to us: the legitimate interest in replying to you and solving what you tell us about (Art. 6.1.f GDPR).

Google processes the AdMob data as an independent controller, under its own policies.

Data stored on your phone remains there until you delete it, uninstall the app or clear its data. This app keeps no data outside your phone, except the messages you send us, which are deleted once the matter is resolved and, at the latest, one year after we receive them; Google keeps its own data according to its policies.

Security

What the app stores is kept in its private storage, which Android does not let other apps access, and it is not uploaded to the cloud backup. The app has no servers or accounts of its own that could suffer a data breach. The backup file is saved only where you choose and has no password, so keep it somewhere you trust. Connections made by Google's ads SDK are encrypted (HTTPS). The messages you send us are kept in an email account that only JR Soft can access, and are deleted within the periods stated above.

Your rights

If you live in the European Union, the GDPR and Spain's Organic Law 3/2018 (LOPDGDD) grant you rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability and objection. In practice, this app keeps none of your data outside your phone except the messages you send us (for those, write to the contact address); AdMob data is processed by Google, with whom you can exercise your rights (policies.google.com/privacy). For what is on your phone:

  • Access and portability: save a backup to a file from Settings → Your data.
  • Erasure: clear the app's data from Android settings or uninstall it. There is no need to ask anyone.
  • Objection and withdrawal of consent: change your advertising choice in Settings → Ad privacy options.

You can also write to the contact address or lodge a complaint with the Spanish Data Protection Agency (Agencia Española de Protección de Datos) or with the data protection authority of your country.

US residents

If you live in a US state with its own privacy law (for example, California, Colorado, Connecticut, Texas or Virginia), you have the right to ask that your personal data not be sold or shared for targeted advertising. You can do this in the app: Settings → Ad privacy options → “Don’t sell or share my data”. From then on, you will only see non-personalized ads. The app does not send sensitive data to anyone; your games stay on your phone. For ads, Google's SDK uses device identifiers and an approximate location (city level). For any other request about your data, write to us at demual@gmail.com.

Residents of Brazil

If you live in Brazil, the General Data Protection Law (LGPD, Law 13,709/2018) gives you the rights of confirmation and access, correction, anonymization, blocking or deletion, portability, information about whom your data is shared with and about the possibility of not giving consent, and withdrawal of consent (Art. 18). The legal bases are those described in “Legal basis and retention”: your consent for personalized advertising (Art. 7, I) and legitimate interest for the rest (Art. 7, IX). AdMob data may be processed outside Brazil, with the safeguards described in “International transfers” (Art. 33). As everywhere else, this app keeps none of your data outside your phone except the messages you send us; for any request, write to demual@gmail.com. You can also lodge a complaint with the National Data Protection Authority (ANPD).

Children

Siempre cabe is not directed at children under 16. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 16; the technical data sent by AdMob is the data described above.
